Unified Malaysian Primary Care Decision Flowchart

Cross-referencing CPG T2DM (6th Ed), CPG HTN (6th Ed, 2026), and CPG Dyslipidemia (6th Ed, 2023)

Step 1

Cardiovascular Risk Tiering

  • Extreme Risk: Established ASCVD with recurrent event (<2y), ASCVD + DM + CKD, or CAD + Familial Hypercholesterolemia.
  • Very High Risk: Documented clinical ASCVD, T2DM with microvascular TOD, severe CKD (eGFR <30), or 10-year FRS >20%.
  • High Risk: T2DM without TOD but duration ≥10y or with ≥1 risk factor, moderate CKD (eGFR 30–59), or severe single risk factor (LDL ≥4.9, BP ≥180/110).
  • Moderate / Low Risk: Young T2DM (<40y) duration <10y without other risk factors.
Step 2

Triple-Target Formulation

  • BP Goal: <130/80 mmHg in all high/very high risk patients; <140/90 in frail elderly. Avoid DBP <60 in IHD.
  • LDL-C Goal: <1.0 (Extreme), <1.4 (Very High), <1.8 (High), <2.6 (Moderate). Minimum 50% drop required from baseline.
  • HbA1c Goal: ≤6.5% for young/new onset without complications; 6.6–7.0% standard; 7.1–8.0% for frail elderly/severe hypoglycemia history.
Step 3

Therapy Escalation & Protection

  • ASCVD / HF / CKD: Prioritize SGLT2i and/or GLP-1 RA regardless of baseline HbA1c.
  • Hypertension: Start Single-Pill Combination (A+C or A+D). ACEi/ARB is mandatory in microalbuminuria.
  • Dyslipidemia: High-intensity statin upfront. Add Ezetimibe 10mg early if not at target at 4–12 weeks.

Bedside ECG Quick Reference for Primary Care NCD Clinics

Key ECG hallmarks in Hypertensive Heart Disease, Diabetic Ischemia, and Electrolyte Derangements

Bedside Recognition

Left Ventricular Hypertrophy (LVH) & Strain

  • Sokolow-Lyon Voltage: $S\text{ in } V_1 + R\text{ in } V_5 \text{ or } V_6 \ge 35\text{ mm}$ (35 small squares).
  • Cornell Voltage: $R\text{ in } aVL + S\text{ in } V_3 > 28\text{ mm}$ (men) or $> 20\text{ mm}$ (women).
  • LV Strain Pattern: Asymmetrical, downsloping ST depression and T-wave inversion in lateral leads (I, aVL, V5, V6).
  • Clinical Action: Represents established target-organ damage. Mandates strict BP target <130/80 mmHg with ACEi/ARB + CCB.

Ischemia & Silent MI in Diabetes

  • Silent Ischemia: Diabetics with autonomic neuropathy often present without chest pain (dyspnea, unexplained sweating).
  • Pathological Q-Waves: Width ≥ 0.04s (1 small square) or depth ≥ 25% of the ensuing R wave in ≥2 contiguous leads.
  • Dynamic ST-T Changes: Horizontal/downsloping ST depression ≥ 0.5 mm or deep symmetrical T-wave inversions.
  • Clinical Action: Upgrades patient automatically to Very High / Extreme Risk. Initiate DAPT, High-intensity Statin, and urgent Cardiology review.

Hyperkalemia (K+ ≥ 5.5 – 6.5+ mmol/L)

Emergency
  • K+ 5.5–6.5: Tall, symmetrical, narrow-based "tented" T waves (most visible in II, III, V2–V4).
  • K+ 6.5–7.5: Prolongation of PR interval, flattening and loss of P waves, ST segment depression.
  • K+ > 7.5: Marked QRS widening progressing into a classic Sine-Wave pattern, VF, or Asystole.
  • GP Action: IV Calcium Gluconate 10% 10 mL over 5 min to stabilize myocardium + IV Dextrose 50% 50 mL with 10U Regular Insulin + Urgent transfer.

Hypokalemia & Drug-Induced Long QTc

  • Hypokalemia (from high-dose loop/thiazide diuretics): T-wave flattening, ST depression, and prominent U waves following T waves.
  • Prolonged QTc Interval: QTc > 450 ms in men, > 460 ms in women. QTc > 500 ms carries high risk of Torsades de Pointes.
  • Pharmacological Alert: Watch for synergistic QT prolongation if co-prescribing macrolides, fluoroquinolones, antipsychotics, or antiemetics.

SADMANS Sick-Day Management Protocol

Preventing Euglycemic DKA, Lactic Acidosis, and Acute Kidney Injury During Acute Illness

Patient Sick-Day Rules (English)

For patients on Diabetes, Blood Pressure, or Kidney medications

WHEN SICK (Fever, Vomiting, Diarrhea, Inability to drink fluids):

TEMPORARILY STOP THESE MEDICATIONS ("SADMANS"):

  • S - Sulfonylureas: Gliclazide, Glimepiride (avoids severe hypoglycemia when not eating).
  • A - ACE-Inhibitors: Perindopril, Ramipril, Enalapril (prevents kidney failure).
  • D - Diuretics: Furosemide, Hydrochlorothiazide, Indapamide (avoids dehydration).
  • M - Metformin: Stop to prevent dangerous Lactic Acidosis.
  • A - ARBs: Losartan, Telmisartan, Valsartan (prevents kidney failure).
  • N - NSAIDs: Ponstan, Ibuprofen, Voltaren, Celebrex (toxic to kidneys when dehydrated).
  • S - SGLT2 Inhibitors: Forxiga (Dapagliflozin), Jardiance (Empagliflozin) - Must stop immediately to avoid Acidosis (Ketoacidosis).

WHAT YOU MUST DO:

  • Check your blood sugar every 4 to 6 hours.
  • Sip plenty of fluids (water, clear soup, ORS) - aim for 1 cup per hour while awake.
  • Restart your regular medications only 24 to 48 hours after you are fully recovered and eating normally.

Peraturan Hari Sakit (Bahasa Melayu)

Untuk pesakit yang mengambil ubat Kencing Manis, Darah Tinggi, atau Buah Pinggang

KETIKA SAKIT (Demam kuat, Muntah-muntah, Cirit-birit, Tidak lalu makan/minum):

HENTIKAN SEMENTARA UBAT-UBATAN INI ("SADMANS"):

  • S - Sulfonilurea: Gliclazide, Glimepiride (elak paras gula jatuh bahaya).
  • A - Perencat ACE: Perindopril, Ramipril, Enalapril (elak buah pinggang rosak).
  • D - Diuretik (Ubat Lawas Kencing): Furosemide, Indapamide (elak kekeringan air).
  • M - Metformin: Hentikan sementara untuk elak keracunan asid laktik.
  • A - ARB: Losartan, Telmisartan, Valsartan (elak kerosakan buah pinggang).
  • N - NSAID (Ubat Tahan Sakit): Ponstan, Voltaren, Ibuprofen (merosakkan buah pinggang).
  • S - Perencat SGLT2: Forxiga, Jardiance - Wajib henti segera bagi mengelakkan komplikasi Ketoasidosis.

TINDAKAN YANG PERLU DIAMBIL:

  • Periksa paras gula darah setiap 4 hingga 6 jam di rumah.
  • Minum air kosong atau sup sedikit demi sedikit untuk mengekalkan hidrasi.
  • Mulakan semula ubat 24 hingga 48 jam selepas anda sembuh sepenuhnya dan boleh makan seperti biasa.
